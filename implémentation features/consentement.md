**Aim of the research project:**

This study focuses on the interactions between an artificial agent and an operator while both collaborate towards a common goal. This research project uses the video game Overcooked to explore this collaboration. More details about our hypothesis and expected results will be provided at the end of the study.

**What is expected of you (methodology):**

If you agree to participate in this study you will take part of an experiment during which you will play to the video game Overcooked. Before the beginning of the experimental session per se, you will be able to practice and learn how to play the game within a tutorial. Several surveys will be presented to you before, during and after the experiment. Those surveys will ask you about general truth or about the particular condition in which you played.

During the task you will be ask to complete several recipes shown on a computer screen. To complete those recipes you will have to move assets (ingredients, plates or meals) in a cutting table, in an exchange area, in a pot, or in the serving area. Upon completion the completed recipe will be removed from the all order list showing every recipe to complete. Your objective is to complete every recipes in a minimum of time.

You will play with an artificial agent which will or won’t communicate with you. After every experimental block, it will be offered to you to have a break. Every block will be constituted by 10 games (trials). The total duration of the study will be approximately 60 minutes.

**Benefits:**
·        _Benefits in terms of scientific advances:_
This study will provide a better understanding of how the communication between an artificial agent and an operator may shape their cooperation, this will provide new insights for future guidelines in the field of Human Autonomy Teaming.
·        _Benefits for society:_
The widespread adoption of artificial intelligence technologies entails more frequent interactions between the general population and increasingly autonomous agents. The manner in which users engage with these systems is crucial to their effective integration. Vocal interaction offers a simple and fluid modality, provided there is mutual understanding between the operator and the artificial agent. While this holds true for civilian applications, it may become even more critical in military contexts.
·        _Benefits for the participant__:_
There is no explicit benefit for participants. However, participants will receive, at their request, a complete explanation about the results of this study once completed.

**Possible risks:**
To the best of our knowledge, this research involves no risks or discomforts other than those encountered in everyday life. This includes watching a screen for the duration of the study, including breaks allowing the participant to rest, preventing dry eyes. All the data collection devices are non-invasive and painless, with no known risks or contraindications.

The study will involve the presentation of continuous visual stimuli on a computer screen. This type of stimuli and the duration of the experiment (approximatively 60 minutes) may induce visual fatigue. Although the fatigue generated will be no greater than that experienced in everyday life by being exposed to a screen for this length of time, a break will be possible between the experimental blocks in order to limit this fatigue and the errors associated with it.
